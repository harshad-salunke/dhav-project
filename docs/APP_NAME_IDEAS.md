# DHAV — App Name Ideas & Branding Research

> Researched based on naming strategies of Zepto, Blinkit, Swiggy, Dunzo, and Zomato.
> Goal: Short, punchy, Indian-rooted, globally pronounceable, memorable, unique.

---

## How Great Indian Startup Names Were Created

| App | Name Origin | Strategy |
|---|---|---|
| **Zepto** | "Zeptosecond" — smallest unit of time | Physics metaphor → speed |
| **Blinkit** | "Blink of an eye" + "it" (done) | Action metaphor → instant |
| **Dunzo** | Slang for "done, over, finished" | Casual/slang → task complete |
| **Swiggy** | Kannada "Suggi" (harvest/food festival) | Cultural root + phonetic fun |
| **Zomato** | "Tomato" with Z twist | Food adjacent + memorable twist |

**Pattern:** Short (1-2 syllables) + Evokes speed/action/locality + Easy to say in Hindi/Marathi/English

## Top 10 Name Suggestions for Your App

---

### 🥇 1. GALLI
**Pronunciation:** GAA-lee  
**Meaning:** "Lane / Street" in Hindi and Marathi — the exact lane where your kirana store is  
**Why it works:**
- Ultra-hyperlocal feel — "your galli has a store"
- Every Indian instantly understands the meaning
- Short, punchy, easy to type and say
- Tagline: *"Your galli, delivered."*
- Domain: galli.in / galliapp.com
- **This is our top pick — it IS the concept.**

---

### 🥈 2. KIRI
**Pronunciation:** KEE-ree  
**Meaning:** Modern short form of "Kirana" — reimagined  
**Why it works:**
- Sounds modern, minimal, global (like "Kiri" cream cheese — known brand)
- Short, brandable, easy to remember
- Phonetically crisp, works across all Indian languages
- Tagline: *"Kiri — your neighbourhood store, now on your phone."*
- Domain: kiriapp.com / getkiri.in
- Could also be positioned as "Kiri = Kirana + री (Marathi feminine ending = familiar/own)"

---

### 🥉 3. DHAV
**Pronunciation:** DHAAV (like a short sprint)  
**Meaning:** "Sprint / Rush" in Hindi and Marathi  
**Why it works:**
- Conveys SPEED perfectly (like Zepto's zeptosecond angle)
- Unique — nothing in market sounds like this
- Sporty, energetic brand feel
- Tagline: *"Dhav — sprint from store to door."*
- Domain: dhavapp.com

---

### 4. TATPAR
**Pronunciation:** TAAT-par  
**Meaning:** "Always ready / Alert / Instant" in Hindi/Sanskrit  
**Why it works:**
- Strong brand meaning — a store that is always ready for you
- More premium-sounding than the others
- Could work for a higher-end expansion
- Tagline: *"Tatpar — your store, always ready."*

---

### 5. JHAPAK
**Pronunciation:** JHA-pak  
**Meaning:** "In a flash / Instantly" in colloquial Hindi  
**Why it works:**
- Very catchy and fun to say
- "Jhapak se aa gaya" (arrived in a flash) — very natural phrase in India
- Communicates speed through everyday language
- Tagline: *"Jhapak — in a flash, at your door."*

---

### 6. NABO
**Pronunciation:** NAY-bo  
**Meaning:** Short for "Naboo / Neighbour" — your neighborhood  
**Why it works:**
- Community and neighborhood feel
- Works globally (sounds like "neighbor" in many languages)
- Short and very easy to remember
- Tagline: *"Nabo — your neighbour's store, at your door."*

---

### 7. BAZIK
**Pronunciation:** BAA-zik  
**Meaning:** "Bazaar + Quick" portmanteau  
**Why it works:**
- Like Grofers → Blinkit, a portmanteau is a proven naming strategy
- "Bazaar" is universally understood across India
- "Quick" embedded in name communicates value proposition
- Tagline: *"Bazik — bazaar speed, doorstep ease."*

---

### 8. DUKKI
**Pronunciation:** DUK-ee  
**Meaning:** Diminutive of "Dukaan" (shop) — affectionate form  
**Why it works:**
- Warm, friendly, very Indian small-store feel
- Like calling a dukaan your own little "dukki"
- Works well for a community-first brand
- Tagline: *"Dukki — your little store, big heart."*

---

### 9. TEZI
**Pronunciation:** TAY-zee  
**Meaning:** "Speed / Sharpness" in Hindi  
**Why it works:**
- One word that means both speed and precision
- Easy across all Indian languages
- Short, punchy, action-oriented
- Tagline: *"Tezi — quick like your kirana chacha."*

---

### 10. CHATAK
**Pronunciation:** CHAA-tak  
**Meaning:** "Sharp / Snappy / Instant" in Hindi — also means a delicious tangy taste  
**Why it works:**
- Double meaning: speed + flavor (great for grocery)
- Very catchy and colloquial
- Tagline: *"Chatak — sharp delivery, fresh taste."*

---

## Final Recommendation

| Rank | Name | Best For |
|---|---|---|
| 🥇 | **GALLI** | Most unique, most Indian, most hyperlocal. Our top choice. |
| 🥈 | **KIRI** | Most modern and globally scalable brand. Great for future expansion. |
| 🥉 | **DHAV** | Best for conveying speed. Strong energy brand. |
| 4th | **TATPAR** | Most premium feel. Good if targeting higher-tier customers. |
| 5th | **JHAPAK** | Most fun and catchy. Great for social media/viral marketing. |
